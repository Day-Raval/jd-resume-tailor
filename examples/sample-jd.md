# Sample JD: AI Engineer

Location: Remote OR Alameda CA / San Ramon CA (Hybrid). 2 positions.
Employment: Full-time preferred; C2C considered for strong fit.

## Job Summary
We are looking for an experienced AI Engineer to design, develop, deploy, and optimize AI/ML solutions that solve business problems and enhance product capabilities. The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience in machine learning, generative AI, data engineering, model deployment, and cloud technologies.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models for business use cases.
- Build and maintain Generative AI (GenAI) and LLM-based applications.
- Develop data pipelines for collecting, cleaning, and preparing training datasets.
- Fine-tune and optimize foundation models for performance and scalability.
- Integrate AI services into production applications and enterprise systems.
- Work closely with Product Managers, Data Engineers, and Development teams to define AI requirements.
- Monitor model performance, conduct retraining, and ensure reliability.
- Implement MLOps practices including model versioning, CI/CD, and monitoring.
- Research and evaluate emerging AI technologies and frameworks.
- Ensure AI solutions align with security, governance, and compliance standards.

## Required Skills
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Machine Learning & Deep Learning frameworks: TensorFlow / PyTorch, Scikit-learn
- Generative AI / LLMs: OpenAI APIs, LangChain / LlamaIndex, Prompt Engineering, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), Fine-tuning and embeddings
- Vector Databases: Pinecone, Weaviate, Chroma, FAISS
- Cloud Platforms: AWS / Azure / GCP
- MLOps tools: Docker, Kubernetes, MLflow, CI/CD pipelines
- APIs and microservices architecture.
- Strong understanding of SQL/NoSQL databases.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Bachelor's/Master's degree in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or related field.
- Experience in deploying AI applications in production.
- Knowledge of NLP, Computer Vision, or Predictive Analytics.
- Exposure to Databricks, Spark, and distributed systems.

## Experience
- 2–3 years overall experience
- 2+ years in AI/ML / GenAI development

## Nice to Have
- Agentic AI / Multi-agent frameworks
- Knowledge Graphs
- AI observability and governance
- Experience with enterprise AI products
